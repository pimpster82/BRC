## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Bible Reading Companion is a web application that helps Jehovah's Witnesses organize and track three independent Bible reading systems. The app operates offline-first with optional Firebase synchronization and integrates seamlessly with JW.org and the JW Library app through deep links.

**Core Principle:** All three reading systems are independent but share a unified progress tracking architecture based on a single `chaptersRead` array.
